# IT3081 Assignment Notes

**Module:** IT3081 Statistical Modelling
**Assignment:** Group Assignment (10%) — "Industry Innovation Consulting
Challenge: From Data to Strategic Decisions"
**Format:** Groups of up to 4 act as an independent statistical consulting
company hired to investigate a business/societal problem. No dataset is
provided — the group must find, justify, and reference their own.

## Assessment
- Consultancy Presentation: 40% (15 minutes)
- Viva Examination: 60% (10 minutes)
- Bonus: +10% for a real industry consultancy engagement; +5% for a
  professional LinkedIn showcase video

## The 12 tasks

1. **Understanding the Industry Problem** — industry background, organizational
   context, problem description, importance, stakeholders, business decisions
   influenced, consultancy objectives
2. **Research Landscape** — literature review: current practices, existing
   statistical approaches, recent findings, strengths/weaknesses of prior
   studies, research gaps, justification for chosen approach.
   Minimum: 15 peer-reviewed papers, 10 journal papers, 5 within the last 5
   years. Must be critical comparison, not summary.
3. **Dataset Understanding & Descriptive Analysis** — dataset description,
   variable descriptions, data quality assessment, missing values, outliers,
   descriptive statistics, visualizations, initial business insights
4. **Statistical Inference** — comparison of means/proportions/variances,
   ANOVA, with hypotheses, justification, results, and practical interpretation
5. **Predictive Statistical Modelling** — multiple linear regression, logistic
   regression, GLM, ridge/LASSO/elastic net, best subset/forward/backward
   selection; justify, compare, evaluate assumptions, recommend a model
6. **Critical Evaluation of Experimental Design** — discuss CRD and RCBD
   suitability (not required to run an actual experiment)
7. **Critical Evaluation of PCA** — whether dimensionality reduction helps this
   dataset
8. **Critical Evaluation of Bayesian Methods** — Naïve Bayes, Bayesian
   regression, Bayesian decision-making
9. **Time Series Analysis** — discussion only; how it could apply if
   longitudinal data existed
10. **Industry Innovation Proposal** — a data-driven solution (dashboard,
    early-warning system, etc.)
11. **Industry Expert Validation** — interview/feedback from someone in the
    field
12. **Final Consultancy Recommendations** — strategic/operational/ethical
    recommendations backed by evidence

## Chosen dataset

`Students_Performance_dataset.csv` — 1,194 real student survey responses,
31 variables, from a South Asian university BCSE (Computer Science &
Engineering) program. Includes `Average attendance on class`, `current CGPA`,
`previous SGPA`, `probation` and `suspension` flags, plus a wide set of
behavioral/socioeconomic predictors.
